# Compute aggregation

Samples are linearly interpolated between consecutive readings of the same pod UID, namespace, group and workload. Intervals longer than five minutes, before the first sample or after the last sample are not estimated. Intervals crossing midnight are split at midnight in Europe/Stockholm, including daylight-saving transitions. CPU is integrated in core-hours and RAM in GiB-hours. The result is an estimate, not a bill: short jobs, outages, rapidly changing loads and samples around startup/shutdown can be missed. Existing measured-window `UsageRecord` rows remain intact and are not added to the new totals, avoiding double counting. Source-neutral CSV ingestion and its inclusion in the calendar remain future work.

The daily chart uses one-minute points from the same interpolation and breaks the entire stack when an in-scope pod has a known gap over five minutes or no pod has interpolated data. Missing pods that never produced a sample cannot be detected. A stacked monthly bar chart uses daily summaries and today's live calculation; group by controller or group and click a bar to open a day. Cost is enabled only when every displayed workload has a rate in the same currency. Dashboard overview figures sum the latest samples no older than five minutes, which may be from different instants, and today's interpolated totals. All figures remain estimates.

The collector records the controlling pod owner and resolves ReplicaSets to Deployments. The service account needs namespace-scoped `list` on apps/replicasets in addition to pods and pod metrics. Deployments, StatefulSets and DaemonSets are grouped by controller, namespace and group; Jobs and unknown owners are isolated by pod UID. This preserves Job attempts separately. Historical samples without ownership metadata remain separate by pod. Never infer ownership from a pod-name suffix. Group labels are unverified and must be reconciled with OMERO before billing.

Run `manage.py aggregate_compute` at 16:50 Europe/Stockholm to rebuild the previous calendar day. The command also accepts `--date YYYY-MM-DD` for a completed day. A transaction replaces only that day's derived rows; source snapshots are retained. Use one scheduler with `concurrencyPolicy: Forbid`, restrict concurrent manual rebuilds, monitor failures and preserve snapshots and daily tables in backups. The aggregation identity requires SELECT on snapshots and SELECT/INSERT/DELETE on `dashboard_compute_dailyusage` plus sequence USAGE; the web identity needs SELECT. The calendar computes the current day on demand, and completed days appear once aggregated. Backfill existing dates explicitly before relying on history. Changing rates retrospectively changes displayed estimates; rate changes within a day make that day's estimates unpriced. Missing rates remain unpriced. Disk remains unavailable. No automatic raw-data deletion or rollup retention is provided.
